Who we are

The name came from the coaching, not a branding meeting.

Together All Prosper started as an online group of independent traders. The message to that group never changed: focus, stay positive, lead the way, work together. Said often enough, it became the name.

Together, we all prosper.

Today it is a small, invite-only school. Each student trades their own account and makes their own decisions. What we share is the method, the practice and the room.

Our code

Four tenets from the dojo floor

In this dojo you are not copying anyone's trades. From your first lesson you are building your own trading playbook: your setups, your rules, your routine, written down and drilled until you can run it without second-guessing. Master Oblywon guides every page of it with The Money Box System. The four tenets are how that playbook gets written, and how it holds up under pressure.

I

Courtesy

Respect the market, the room and every trader in it.

II

Modesty

Start small. Let your reps, not your ego, size your trades.

III

Integrity

Trade the plan you wrote. Log every trade honestly.

IV

Indomitable Spirit

A losing day is a lesson. Journal it. Reflect. Back on the mat tomorrow.

A master without a dojo is a master without borders. The whole world is the school.
